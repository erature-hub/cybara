You're connected

Cybara can now work in the tabs you share. Here's how to use it.

1

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Cybara Browser Bridge so it's always one click away.

2

Share a tab

On any website, click the Cybara icon and turn on Share. Turn it off any time to take the tab back.

3

Just ask

Tell any Cybara agent "use my Chrome to…". It works in your shared tab or opens its own in a Cybara tab group.

Agents only reach tabs you share or tabs Cybara opens. Chrome shows a banner while a tab is being controlled, and choosing Cancel there stops it immediately.
